Syncing with your TV

  1. 1Open the delay control from the Live chip in the top bar.
  2. 2Pick a moment you can see on both: the lap counter ticking over, or the session clock in practice and qualifying.
  3. 3Add seconds until Pitwall changes at the same moment as the TV. While the buffer fills you will see Syncing.

What the data can't do

Map positions are estimates. The free feed has no GPS, so cars are placed from about 20 mini-sectors per lap and can jump. No car telemetry. Speed, throttle and gear need F1 TV. Transcripts can be wrong. Team radio is transcribed automatically. Long-run pace ignores fuel. Fuel loads are not published, so compare long runs with care.
